Section 01

The Problem Nobody Is Talking About

There is a knowledge crisis hiding in plain sight.

It is not that information is scarce. If anything, information is overwhelming. The real problem is that most people cannot access the kind of thinking that actually changes their decisions.

Let me explain what I mean.

The Gap Between Information and Insight

When you are facing a hard problem: a business decision, a creative block, a question about your field: you do not need more articles. You need to think alongside someone who has been there. Someone who has developed a point of view through years of experience, failure, and refinement.

The people who think at that level are rare. And they are inaccessible to most people.

If you want to learn from Marcus Aurelius, you read Meditations: a filtered, translated, edited version of his journal, stripped of the back-and-forth that makes learning real. If you want to talk to Richard Feynman, you watch lectures. Monologues. Performances. Not conversations.

And if you want to access the thinking of a living expert: a founder who has built what you are trying to build, a scientist in the exact field you are studying: you are usually blocked by time, cost, geography, or access.

The best thinking in human history is locked behind static formats. Books, lectures, articles. Formats designed to broadcast, not to converse.

Myndzprint exists to change that. Not to replace those formats. To extend them: into something alive, responsive, and genuinely useful.

Why This Matters More Than Ever

We live in a moment where AI is promising to democratise knowledge. And it is: to a point. General AI assistants like ChatGPT and Claude can answer questions across any domain. They are extraordinary tools.

But they have a fundamental limitation: they are generalised. They do not think like Marcus Aurelius thought. They do not approach a business problem the way Charlie Munger would. They synthesise: they do not embody.

The difference between a generalised answer and an expert's answer is not just accuracy. It is perspective. Mental models. The particular lens through which a thinker filters reality.

That is what we are building. Not another assistant. A platform for perspective.

Section 02

What Myndzprint Actually Is

Myndzprint is an AI minds platform.

A mind on Myndzprint is a conversational AI built to think, respond, and reason like a specific person: a historical figure, a domain expert, a living thinker, or yourself.

It is not a chatbot. It is not a search engine. It is not a knowledge base with a chat interface bolted on.

It is the closest thing to sitting across the table from a thinker and asking them anything.

The Core Concept: A Mind

When we use the word "mind" at Myndzprint, we mean it precisely.

A mind is built from the actual material that shaped a thinker's worldview: their writing, their speeches, their interviews, their frameworks, their stated principles. We feed that material into a purpose-built model and the result is a version of that thinker: one that can engage in real, coherent, nuanced conversation.

Ask Marcus Aurelius about leadership under pressure. Ask Naval Ravikant about your startup pricing strategy. Ask Wole Soyinka about the themes in your essay. Ask a version of yourself: trained on your own notes and experiences: what you actually believe about a problem you're wrestling with.

Every response stays grounded in how that person actually thought. It is not hallucination. It is simulation backed by source material.

The Three Types of Minds

Historical Figures

The thinkers, leaders, and creators who shaped human civilisation. Philosophers, scientists, strategists, artists. People whose ideas remain relevant centuries after their death: now available for direct conversation.

Examples: Marcus Aurelius, Ada Lovelace, Nikola Tesla, Chinua Achebe, Sun Tzu.

Living Experts

Domain experts and thought leaders who are actively shaping their fields. Entrepreneurs, academics, investors, creatives. People whose perspectives are valuable but whose time is scarce.

Examples: A leading oncologist. A startup founder who has exited twice. An economist specialising in African markets.

The third type of mind: and perhaps the most powerful: is you.

Your Own Mind

Myndzprint lets you build a mind of yourself.

Upload your notes, your writing, your voice recordings, your frameworks. Train a version of you that has access to everything you have thought about and written over time. Then use it: to reflect, to pressure-test ideas, to onboard teammates into how you think, or to share your perspective at scale.

Most people carry more insight than they can access in the moment. A mind of yourself is a way to never lose that thinking.

For founders, consultants, educators, and creators, this is not a gimmick. It is a genuine productivity and legacy tool.

Section 03

How It Works

The technology behind Myndzprint is sophisticated. The experience should feel simple.

Here is how a mind is built and how it functions.

Step 1: Source Material

Every mind begins with source material: the raw intellectual output of the person being built. This includes:

  • Books, essays, and published writing
  • Speeches, interviews, and transcripts
  • Letters, journals, and private correspondence
  • Recorded lectures and documented frameworks
  • For living subjects or the user themselves: uploaded notes, voice recordings, and documents

Step 2: Mind Construction

The source material is processed through Myndzprint's mind-building pipeline. This is not simple fine-tuning. We are extracting and encoding:

  • Characteristic vocabulary and linguistic patterns
  • Core beliefs and stated principles
  • Typical mental models and reasoning structures
  • Known positions on key topics
  • The emotional register and tone of the thinker

The result is a model that does not just know what the person knew: it approximates how they would think about something new.

Step 3: Conversation

Users interact with minds through a natural conversation interface. No prompts required. No instructions. Just questions.

The mind responds in character: drawing on its source material, maintaining consistency with the thinker's known worldview, and flagging where it is speculating beyond its source.

Conversations can be deep. A user might spend thirty minutes working through a business problem with a version of Charlie Munger. Or a student might interrogate Plato's arguments on justice across three sessions.

The measure of a good mind is not whether it gives you information. It is whether it changes how you think.

Section 05

Why This Moment

This is not a product that could have been built five years ago. Three converging forces have made it possible now.

1. The Foundation Model Moment

The emergence of large language models: GPT-4, Claude, Gemini and others: has created a foundation that did not exist before. These models can maintain character, sustain long context, reason across complex topics, and adapt to nuanced prompting.

We are not building the model. We are building on top of the model: and that is the right decision. Our value is in what we layer on top: the mind construction methodology, the source material curation, the conversation design, and the product experience.

2. The Content Abundance Problem

There has never been more content in human history. And there has never been a harder time to find the thinking that actually matters.

Myndzprint is not adding to the noise. It is converting the best existing content into interactive intelligence. Every book, every speech, every essay that goes into a mind becomes more valuable: not less: because it can now be accessed in conversation rather than in isolation.

3. The Democratisation of Access

There is a profound inequality in the world of ideas. People with the right networks and the right institutions get access to the best thinkers. Everyone else gets filtered, packaged, secondhand versions.

Myndzprint is an access play. A student in Lagos should be able to think alongside the same minds as a student at Harvard. A first-generation entrepreneur should have access to the same calibre of perspective as someone whose parents ran companies.

Myndzprint does not level the playing field. It changes the game entirely.

Section 07

The Product, In Detail

The Conversation Interface

The primary interface is a clean, focused conversation. No clutter. No sidebar of features competing for attention. Just you and the mind.

The design philosophy is deliberate: we want the conversation to feel different from a Google search or a ChatGPT session. It should feel like sitting down with someone.

We have invested in the quality of the conversation experience: the pacing, the response structure, the way the mind maintains character over a long session. Small things that compound into a feeling of genuine engagement.

The Mind Library

Myndzprint will host a growing library of pre-built minds: curated, quality-controlled, and regularly updated as new source material is available.

These are not quick builds. Each mind goes through a construction and review process. We would rather have fewer minds that are genuinely good than many minds that are shallow.

Build Your Own Mind

Users and organisations can build minds through the Myndzprint platform. The process is:

  • Upload source material: documents, transcripts, audio, notes
  • Define the scope and parameters of the mind
  • Review and test through a conversation interface
  • Publish privately (personal use), to an organisation, or publicly to the Myndzprint library

Memory and Learning (Roadmap)

The next phase of the product introduces persistent memory per mind. Every conversation with a mind builds a shared history: the mind learns what you care about, what you have already covered, and how your thinking has evolved.

This transforms Myndzprint from a conversation tool into a genuine thinking partnership over time.

We are also building wiki-style knowledge layers per mind: structured knowledge that grows as users interact, building a living document of what the mind knows and has been taught.

Section 09

Ethics and Trust

We do not take this lightly.

Building minds of real people: living or historical: comes with serious responsibilities. Let me be explicit about how we think about them.

Consent and Permissions

For living subjects, Myndzprint operates with explicit consent. No mind of a living person is built without their participation or the authorisation of a legitimate rights holder.

For historical figures, we work within the public domain and are guided by scholarly standards: using only publicly available, verified source material.

Accuracy and Transparency

A mind is not perfect. It is an approximation. We are transparent about this: every mind interaction includes clear labelling that users are engaging with an AI-powered simulation, not the person themselves.

Where a mind is speculating beyond its source material, it says so. We are building in uncertainty disclosure as a product feature, not hiding it.

Misuse Prevention

We have strict guardrails against generating content that would defame, misrepresent, or harm the subjects of our minds. No mind will be weaponised to put damaging words in someone's mouth.

Our moderation and audit infrastructure is built from the beginning, not bolted on after the fact.

Trust is the product. Everything else is the means of delivering it.
